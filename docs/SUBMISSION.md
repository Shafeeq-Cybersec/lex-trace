# Submission preparation

## Short description
TRACE helps renters review security-deposit deductions using scattered PDFs, messages, receipts and photographs. It shows each claim, the records that support or challenge it, and the evidence still needed. Findings link to source pages. New evidence produces a separate revision showing what changed, while preserving the earlier review.

## GenAI contribution
Gemini creates structured cross-record assessments and checks their semantic support. Native text extraction, exact quotation validation, source access, duplicate suppression, job publication and revision comparison are deterministic. Images and scanned records use explicitly labeled model observation/transcription. The prepared example uses no AI and is labeled accordingly.

## Four-minute demonstration
0:00–0:20: “A deposit deduction is not one document. It is a claim spread across records. TRACE makes that claim inspectable.”
0:20–0:55: Open a previously completed **live** review of the fictional sample packet. State it is a saved real model run. Show the actual deposit, proposed deductions and uncertain assessment.
0:55–1:30: Open the repainting claim citation and move-in PDF. Explain that old marks challenge a blanket new-damage claim but do not prove the deduction invalid.
1:30–2:00: Add the new packing message. Say what it contains before processing; do not imply the outcome is predetermined.
2:00–2:45: During real reassessment, show the old revision and AI audit. Explain exact quote checks and separate semantic verification. If provider latency extends beyond the slot, disclose it.
2:45–3:25: Open the new review and What changed. Verify the additional mark's source, show the old review remains intact, and distinguish damage evidence from repair cost/necessity.
3:25–3:45: Open the preparation brief and evidence gaps. This is an organized record to inspect or bring to a professional.
3:45–4:00: “TRACE does not decide who wins. It makes what changed—and why—possible to verify.”

Fallback: use the clearly labeled prepared example if the provider is unavailable. Say explicitly that its results are prepared and no live AI is running. Never present that as successful live inference.
